Pathological Root-Finding Application

Setup
- Solve `L2 + 0.05 sin(5 L2) = 5`, where `L2 = log(log(x))`.
- Compare Newton directly in `x` against Newton in `L2` space.

Results
- Raw x-space Newton: 25 iterations, final |f|=7.999609e-03, final x=2.419146e+64, final L2=4.998892e+00
- L2-space Newton: 5 iterations, final |f|=6.217249e-15, final x=6.275410e+64, final L2=5.005302e+00

Interpretation
- The raw solver pays the `1 / (x log x)` conditioning penalty, so steps are numerically awkward in raw coordinates.
- The L2-space solver works on the natural comparability class of the equation and therefore converges in a much better-conditioned coordinate system.
