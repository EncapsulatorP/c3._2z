Prime layers that all keep Ω

    One tower layer sets a 90° phase step. An ideal modulator repeats the chosen word on a continuous laser. The bars show the resulting carrier and sideband powers.

    Ideal rectangular phase slots. Power is relative to the input laser. The red bar is the carrier. A whole-word mirror swaps the positive and negative sidebands. A one-slot mirror changes a single Fourier contribution per line. A digit rule updates every changed occurrence.

    Digit rule. Tower height is the number of prime factors of the digit, counting multiplicity. We assign height 0 to the digit 0 by convention, and to 1 because its prime factorization is empty. The cap is 2, so only 8 loses a layer.

    Decimal rule. If the reduced denominator is 2a5bq with q coprime to 10, a recurring expansion has max(a,b) prefix places and repeats after the multiplicative order of 10 modulo q. The tree computes the exact digits by long division.
