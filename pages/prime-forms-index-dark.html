    Ω towers: one fixed point, many stabilities

    The Order of Waves

    The 5 → 7 and 7 → 5 layers share Ω and the same first derivative. Under a small sinusoidal drive their half-difference starts at quadratic order. Subtract its mean to see a leading oscillation at twice the drive frequency. Reversing the layers reverses its sign.
